Palmtide Isle is an original tropical-island open-world crime game: play Raka, a harbour courier caught between the Tide Runners and the Coral Kings. Explore a 3600×2600 island on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the police across five wanted levels, fight gang members, and finish six story missions — from delivering packages at the harbour to a final showdown on the east pier. Top-down 2D canvas, vanilla JavaScript, no libraries and no network requests; all sound is synthesised with WebAudio. HUD with money, health, wanted stars and a live minimap; progress saves to localStorage.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD to walk and drive, Shift to run, E to enter or leave a car, F to punch, Q to start a mission, P to pause, M to mute.
